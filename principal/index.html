02 / Flagship cases

Three systems. Three durable decisions.

Each case is grounded in work from my career record. Results stay qualitative where the supplied record does not include defensible numbers.

ShippedElectronic Arts · Gaming

Make title onboarding configuration, not construction.

I shaped a shared path for game telemetry that moved repeated ingestion work into metadata, contracts and platform controls. Title teams kept their domain meaning; the platform absorbed the undifferentiated engineering.

Open full case

Context & my role

Architecture direction for a shared data path serving game analytics. I stayed close to the first hard implementation path through reference designs, prototypes, Python and SQL.

Business problem

Every bespoke onboarding path multiplied delivery risk, operating burden and the time before a title could use trusted telemetry.

Constraints

Different title semantics, evolving event contracts, multiple cloud storage paths, orchestration, replay, access controls and downstream analytical models.

Key decisions

  • Configuration-led onboarding over hand-built pipelines.
  • Shared contracts and controls without centralizing domain meaning.
  • Replayable object storage and open processing boundaries.

The hard part

Standardization had to remove repeated engineering without erasing the context that made each title’s events useful.

Rollout & risk

Explicit contracts, observable stages and replayable boundaries isolated change and recovery.

Result

Title onboarding moved toward a reusable platform path with common controls, clearer boundaries and less one-off construction.

What others reused

Configuration patterns, orchestration conventions, contract checks and the reference path to governed analytical models.

What I would do differently

Make ownership and unit-cost signals part of the onboarding contract earlier.

ShippedElectronic Arts · Lakehouse

Open the lakehouse without reopening risk.

I worked on Apache Iceberg modernization and Snowflake interoperability to separate durable data from a single engine. The architecture treated migration safety, table maintenance and operating ownership as part of the product.

Open full case

Context & my role

Architecture and trade-off analysis across open-table processing, object storage and warehouse consumption, with hands-on work where uncertainty was highest.

Business problem

The platform needed more interoperability and workload choice without weakening governed access or turning open tables into unmanaged storage.

Constraints

Snapshot semantics, schema and partition evolution, file sizing, metadata growth, reader compatibility, rollback and coexistence with Snowflake.

Key decisions

  • Open tables as a durable boundary, not a migration slogan.
  • Operational ownership for compaction and metadata maintenance.
  • Workload-by-workload migration instead of a forced universal cutover.

The hard part

Open storage increases choice, but also moves responsibility into table maintenance, catalog behavior and cross-engine semantics.

Rollout & risk

Parity checks, reader compatibility, rollback points and explicit workload placement protected the migration.

Result

The platform gained a credible open-table path while preserving governed warehouse access and making operational ownership explicit.

What others reused

Decision criteria for workload placement, migration reviews and operating expectations around open tables.

What I would do differently

Define maintenance budgets and exit criteria with the first table contract.

ShippedCognizant · Greenfield product

Start loyalty from the transaction grain.

I worked on a greenfield loyalty application at Cognizant. Starting from a clean sheet reinforced a rule I still use: member identity and transaction grain are product decisions before they are implementation details.

Open full case

Context & my role

Architecture and engineering work for a greenfield loyalty application within my Cognizant career.

Business problem

A loyalty product has to connect member identity, earning, redemption and service behavior without creating contradictory balances or journeys.

Clean-sheet constraints

The first definitions of identity, transaction grain and ownership become long-lived product boundaries.

Technical judgment

  • Define the member and transaction grain before optimizing flows.
  • Separate durable business rules from channel behavior.
  • Make reconciliation and auditability first-class concerns.

The hard part

Greenfield freedom is temporary. Early shortcuts in identity and transaction semantics constrain every later channel.

Risk management

Reconciliation, ownership and auditable state changes keep the loyalty promise credible.

Result

The engagement established a durable architecture lesson that shapes how I approach identity, contracts and business grain.

What others reused

A model-first frame: define the promise, identity and transaction boundary before the implementation path.

What I would do differently

Write the domain contract as a product artifact from day one.
